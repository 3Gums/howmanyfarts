How Many Farts a Day Is Normal? 💨

The science-backed answer to your gassy questions!

The Quick Answer 🎯

Most people fart between 8–21 times per day. Up to 25 times a day can still be completely normal!

Flatulence is a normal biological function. The amount varies based on diet, digestion speed, and individual gut bacteria. Everyone's butt has its own unique "fart signature."

Why Do We Fart?

Your gut produces gas as it breaks down food. This gas needs to escape somehow—and that's what flatulence is. It's your body working hard to digest your dinner!

💡 Fun Fact: The average fart travels through the air at 7 mph and contains gases like nitrogen, hydrogen, carbon dioxide, and sometimes methane—which is flammable!
